Privacy

Your saved classes and parking location stay in this browser’s local storage. You can remove them using the app’s controls or your browser settings.

The interactive map loads tiles and map assets from OpenFreeMap. See OpenFreeMap’s privacy policy. Campus searches run locally. Location is requested only when you choose to locate yourself. External walking directions open Google Maps and may include your chosen origin and destination.

This site uses Vercel Analytics for basic website usage analytics. See Vercel’s analytics privacy information. External Google Maps links use its Privacy Policy.
